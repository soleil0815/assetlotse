Groups link items and people, e.g. a department, a project or a working group. Every item belongs to one group or is general.

  • Receive: staff members can only be issued items of their groups and general items.
  • See in the inventory: {% if f.catalog_own %}currently staff members see no inventory at all, only their own items; managers see their groups' items{% elif f.catalog_all %}currently everyone sees the whole inventory{% else %}currently only the items of one's own groups and general items{% endif %} – changeable under Settings → Inventory visibility.
  • Managers with a group only look after the items and loans of their groups (plus general ones). A manager without a group is responsible for the whole inventory – e.g. IT.
  1. Administration → Groups → Group: enter name, colour and description.
  2. Tick users on the right under Members – or choose the groups in the user's account.
  3. Save.

Automatically from Active Directory: enter the name (e.g. Dept-Physics) or DN of a directory group under AD group (optional). Its members are assigned to this group at every sign-in and removed again when they leave it. For this, Assign groups automatically must be switched on under Active Directory. Groups assigned manually without a directory link are not affected.

Deleting a group turns its items into general items – nothing is lost.
