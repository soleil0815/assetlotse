My items lists everything currently issued to you, with the issue date and – if agreed – the return date. The Overdue and History tabs show only overdue or already returned items. Where there is a return date, the colour shows the status: green = time left, yellow = due soon, red = overdue. Items issued open-ended are never overdue.

Forgot a key? If the managers have allowed it for a device (e.g. an encrypted drive), its item page has a password field under Confidential details. After entering your password you see e.g. the BitLocker recovery key – only while the device is issued to you. Every view is logged.
