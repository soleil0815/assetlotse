Authenticator app (e.g. Microsoft/Google Authenticator, Aegis, 2FAS):

  1. Click Set up app and scan the QR code (or type in the key).
  2. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and click Confirm.
  3. Write down or print the 10 recovery codes – they are shown only now, and each works once.

Security key (YubiKey or another FIDO2 key, as the second factor after the password): enter a name, click Add and touch the key. This only works over HTTPS and only at the address where the key was registered. Registering a spare key straight away is a good idea.{% if not f.https %} You are not connected over HTTPS right now – only the app works here.{% endif %}

Running out of codes? Generate new codes creates fresh ones – the old ones stop working.

Lost your phone? Under Signed-in devices, Sign out on all other devices signs you out everywhere else, including “Stay signed in”. Also delete the passkey of the lost device (bin icon). You can then sign in with a recovery code – or ask an administrator to reset your two-factor authentication; that also removes all passkeys.
