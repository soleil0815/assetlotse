Under Administration → Branding you adapt AssetLotse to your corporate design. The preview updates instantly; View sign-in page shows the result as visitors see it.

  • Logo (ideally PNG with a transparent background, or SVG) – appears in the sidebar, on the sign-in page, in emails and as the browser icon. Options: Show name next to the logo (switch off if the logo already contains the name) and Show logo on a light background (for dark logos). SVGs with scripts or external references are rejected.
  • Colours: Main colour (buttons, links), Accent colour, Sidebar. Text colours are chosen automatically to stay readable, in dark mode too. Default resets a colour.
  • Sign-in page: Background image (landscape, at least 1600 px; it is scaled down and GPS data is removed), darkening via a slider, Show shelf graphic, Small heading and Welcome text (empty = hidden).
  • Name (organisation or program) – e.g. your institute's name; can also be changed under Settings.

Apply with Save; Reset everything restores logo, image, colours and texts to their defaults. Emails pick up the logo and colours automatically.

Note: the logo and background image can be fetched without signing in because the sign-in page needs them – don't use anything confidential. The files live in ./data/branding and are kept across updates.
