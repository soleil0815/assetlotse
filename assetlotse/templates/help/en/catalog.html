Inventory shows every item you are allowed to see. Matching items appear as you type in the search box at the top – a click (or ↓ and Enter) opens the item, Show all results or Enter the full list. The search box searches name, category, manufacturer, model, serial number, inventory number, location and description – you can also scan an inventory number from its label with a barcode scanner.

  • Filter by category, group, location and status (Available / Issued).
  • Sort by name, category, inventory number or most recently added.
  • View: on the right, switch between Tiles with photos and a compact List. Your browser remembers the choice.
  • Clicking a photo or name opens the item page with location, inventory and serial number, accessories and the number of pieces available.
  • For identical things without a number (e.g. HDMI cables) it shows how many of how many pcs are currently free.
