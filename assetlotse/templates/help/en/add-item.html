1. In the Inventory, click Add item (or + Item at the top).
  2. Enter the name (required, e.g. “Laptop Dell Latitude 5440”) and choose a category. Existing categories are suggested; you create a new one simply by typing it.
  3. Add manufacturer, model, inventory number, serial number as needed, and under Description / accessories e.g. “with charger and bag”. You can scan the numbers straight into the fields with a barcode scanner. Name, manufacturer and model are suggested from the existing inventory; if you pick a known name or model (e.g. “Vostro 3400”), empty fields such as manufacturer and category are filled in to match.
  4. On the right, under Inventory, choose the group (General – for everyone = anyone may receive it), the quantity and the location; optionally add a photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF – it is re-saved and metadata is removed).
  5. Save – or Save & next: a new form opens straight away with the same details and the same photo (without numbers and confidential details). Ideal for ten identical laptops: just scan the inventory and serial number of each.
  • Quantity: 1 for single pieces with their own number. Use more only for identical things without a number, e.g. 20 HDMI cables – they are then issued piece by piece. The quantity cannot drop below the number of pcs currently issued.
  • Numbers are unique: each inventory and serial number exists only once (case-insensitive). While you type, the field shows Available or Already in use (with a link to the item), similar existing inventory numbers and the Next free one – click it to take it over. A number already in use is rejected when saving; your input stays in the form. Duplicates from older versions do not block editing, a warning appears instead. Is a number on another device by mistake? Then, after saving, the form offers Remove it from “…” and enter it here – tick it and save again. The number is removed there and entered here; then enter the correct one on the other device. This also fixes two swapped numbers without creating a device anew.
  • Add similar: on the item page, Add similar creates a new item with the same details – with the photo, but without inventory/serial number and confidential details. To leave the photo out, tick Don't copy the template's photo.
  • Location: if locations are defined under Locations, you pick from a list; otherwise you type freely.

Confidential details (e.g. BitLocker recovery key, PIN, password): enter them in the Confidential details box. They are stored encrypted and never exported. Only administrators and managers who are members of the item's group can see them – for “General”, administrators only. So assign devices with a BitLocker key to a group (e.g. “IT”). On the item page, Reveal displays them (with Copy); every reveal, change and deletion is recorded with name and time in the Log. When editing, the field stays empty – only fill it in to replace the details; tick Delete confidential details to remove them.

Sharing with the borrower: with the box The person this item is issued to may view the details themselves ticked, someone who has forgotten the BitLocker key of their drive can look it up. This applies only to the person who currently has the item and ends when it is returned. They must enter their password first; every view is logged as “viewed (borrower)”. This works only for single items and only if the item was issued by you (or someone else who may see the details) – not via self-checkout. Everyone else still sees nothing. Only people who may see the details themselves can tick or untick the box – this is logged too.

Editing and deleting: the item page has Edit and Delete. An issued item cannot be deleted. Deleting an item also removes its loan history and confidential details.
