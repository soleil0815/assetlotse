The Issue desk is made for many issues and returns in a row, e.g. when new staff members receive their equipment: the input field stays active, so you simply scan one label after another.

Issuing (Issuing tab):

  1. Person: type a username or name – matching accounts{% if f.ldap_cache %} and people from AD (AD – no account yet; a click takes the account over){% endif %} appear as you type – or click Select. On the right you see what the person already has; Change starts with the next person. If the text matches several people, you pick from a list.{% if f.ldap_on and f.ldap_service_account %} Active Directory: the list also shows people from AD who have never signed in to AssetLotse – but only those who would have access under the AD rules (OUs/groups for staff, managers, administrators); accounts disabled or expired in AD are not shown. Take over creates the account (it is checked again directly in AD) – initially as a staff member; manager or admin rights only apply from the person's first own sign-in. For very general search terms “please search more precisely” appears.{% endif %}
  2. Item: scan or type the inventory number, serial number or name and click Issue. While you type (e.g. “drive”), matching items the person may receive appear with “x of y free” – a click (or ↓ and Enter) issues straight away. A barcode scanner is not affected: Enter without a selected suggestion looks up the scanned number as before. Inventory and serial numbers match regardless of upper or lower case. Enter the internal number (shown in the item page's address, e.g. …/gegenstaende/12) with a leading #, i.e. #12.

Issuing from the inventory: in the Inventory (list view, column “Issued to”) and on an item's page, Issue or Find person brings you here with the item already chosen. Pick the person (also from AD), then just confirm Issue “…” to …; afterwards you are taken back to the item.

Groups without lending: items of a group with No lending are not offered here and cannot be issued – checking them in still works.

Search the inventory: if you have no label at hand or are not sure which device is free, use Search the inventory below the scan line: enter search text (name, manufacturer, model, inventory or serial number) and/or pick a category. By default only available items you may issue are shown; choose All to include issued ones. Issue in the result list issues directly to the selected person – the search stays in place so you can pick the next item right away.

With Issue immediately if there is exactly one match (on by default), the item is issued without another click when exactly one matches – the field is ready for the next one straight away. If several match, a list of results appears, each with its own Issue button.

The program automatically checks that the account is active, that the person may receive the item (group), that a piece is still available and that they do not already have it{% if f.max_loans %}, and whether the limit of {{ f.max_loans }} items has been reached{% endif %}. {% if f.loan_days %}The item has to be returned after {{ f.loan_days }} {{ "day" if f.loan_days == 1 else "days" }}.{% else %}Items are issued open-ended.{% endif %} To set a different return date, use Issue to … on the item page.{% if f.mail_on and f.loan_mail %} The person gets a confirmation by email.{% endif %}

Checking in (Return tab): scan the label. With Return immediately if there is exactly one match the loan is closed at once if exactly one is open; otherwise (e.g. several pieces of a cable) pick the right one and click Check in. Quickly check the accessories listed in the note.
