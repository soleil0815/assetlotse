USB or Bluetooth barcode scanners work without any setup: they behave like a keyboard. Click into the input field and scan – the scanner “types” the number from the label and presses Enter. This works in the Inventory search, in the number fields under Add item and at the Issue desk. At the desk a scanner is the fastest and most reliable option.

  • If nothing arrives, the cursor is not in the field – click into it once.
  • If no Enter follows, configure the scanner to send “Enter/CR as suffix” (see its manual).
  • If wrong characters appear (e.g. IT?0042 instead of IT-0042, or Y and Z swapped), the scanner's keyboard layout does not match the computer (e.g. US instead of German).
  • Many devices already carry a manufacturer barcode with the serial number – you can scan that too.

Inventory labels: ideally, stick a label on every item showing the inventory number as a barcode and also in plain text.

  • AssetLotse prints the labels itself: on an item page, Label creates a PDF in the 62 × 29 mm format (Brother QL, e.g. DK-11209 or 62 mm continuous tape). In the Inventory, Labels prints every item of the current selection at once – so filter first, e.g. by category. In the print dialog choose the label size and “Actual size” (100 %).
  • The barcode is Code 128 with the inventory number. If an item has no inventory number (or it contains umlauts), the barcode holds # plus the internal number – the issue desk still finds it.
  • A consistent scheme helps, e.g. a prefix per category plus a running number: LT-0001 for laptops, PR-0001 for projectors.
  • Laminated or polyester labels last longer on laptops and chargers than paper. Keep vents and the rating plate clear.
  • For small parts without their own number (cables, adapters) one shared label on the box or shelf is enough.
